## Method

Four phases:

- Rethink: the Leverage Assessment maps how work actually flows, what must stay in the owner's hands, and what steals hours.
- Build: agents are shaped to the firm's process and voice. Every install has a goal written before the first line of code, with fixed scope and fixed price. First agent live in 30 to 45 days.
- Operate: the system runs every day through human review, an approval ledger, a monthly working session on the numbers, and a new agent every quarter.
- Train: the team learns to run, correct and extend the system through operator sessions, coaching and documented skill progression.

The approval ledger is a queue. Every message, quote and post lands in one place. The owner reads, taps yes, and it ships. A no kills it, and the system learns why. Every decision is logged, timestamped and inspectable.

The success gate is a number, not a vibe. Before building, define the metric, threshold and date. The gate is signed before work starts, reviewed monthly, and decides whether the next phase has been earned.

## FAQ

Q: Is this a sales pitch in disguise?
A: It is a diagnostic with a guarantee attached. If the map is weak, the fee is free and the client keeps the map. The assessment sells the install only if the numbers do.

Q: We are not technical.
A: The client brings process knowledge. We bring the build. The team needs zero code, one messaging app and opinions.

Q: Where does our data sit?
A: In a private, secured environment per client. Data stays theirs, models are theirs if preferred, and there is no lock-in. This is put in writing before seeing a single file.

Q: We already tried AI and it wrote like a robot.
A: A tool nobody operates dies in three weeks. That is an orphan failure, not an AI failure. Operating is the third phase of the method.

Q: Why not just ChatGPT?
A: The choice is between evenings spent prompt-engineering and approving finished work in ten minutes a day.

Q: AI employees are generic spam.
A: Off-the-shelf agents write like no one and sign in the owner's name. The method maps the process first, shapes the agent to it, and keeps the covenant that nothing ships without the owner's yes.

Q: I want volume, unsupervised.
A: This is not for firms that want AI to replace judgment or send volume spam with the owner's signature. The honest route is to stop here.

Q: What if it misses the number?
A: The team fixes it on its time until it passes, or says plainly that it was wrong, and the partnership does not start. The next step is to define the number and threshold.
